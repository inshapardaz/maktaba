# Reading Analytics

Maktaba quietly tracks how much time you actually spend reading, then turns that into a report you
can check any time. Open it from the sidebar's footer — click the chart icon next to Settings.

## How reading time is tracked

While a book is open in the reader and its window is visible, Maktaba keeps a running tally of the
time spent on it. You don't need to start or stop anything — just read, and it's recorded
automatically in the background. Nothing is sent anywhere; it stays in your library alongside
everything else.

## What the report shows

- **Total time read**, **Currently reading**, **Unread**, and **Finished** — a quick summary across
  your whole library, with an estimate of how much time is left on the books you're partway
  through.
- **Most active day** and **most active time** — the day of the week and hour of the day you read
  the most overall.
- **Reading time** — a bar chart of how much you read, switchable between **Daily** (the last 30
  days), **Weekly** (the last 12 weeks), and **Monthly** (the last 12 months). Hover any bar to see
  the exact amount for that period.
- **By day of week** and **By time of day** — two more bar charts showing which days and which
  hours you tend to read at, with your most active day/hour highlighted.
- **By book** — a table of every book you're reading or have finished, with its progress, time
  read so far, and an estimated time remaining. Books you haven't started yet aren't listed here
  since there's nothing to show for them.

## About the time estimates

Maktaba doesn't know how long a book "should" take — instead, once you've read enough of a book to
know your pace on it, it estimates the total from your own reading speed so far. Until a book has
enough of its own data, Maktaba falls back to your average pace across the rest of your library.
These estimates get more accurate the more you read, and will naturally shift as your pace does.
