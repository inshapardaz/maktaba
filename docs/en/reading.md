# Reading Books

Click any book's cover, then click **Read** to open it. Maktaba opens the book in its own reading
window, separate from your library window, so you can keep browsing your library while a book
stays open — you can even have several books open for reading at the same time.

## Reader controls

Inside the reader you can:

- Turn pages, jump to a chapter, and search inside the book.
- Adjust text size, font, and the reading theme (light, dark, sepia, and more) to whatever is
  most comfortable for you.
- Close the reader window at any time — your place is remembered, so the book opens back to the
  same page next time.

## Marking your reading status

From a book's details, you can mark it as **Unread**, **Reading**, or **Finished**. This helps
you keep track of what you're partway through and filter your library to just what you're
currently reading.

## Looking up word meanings

If you've set up an offline dictionary for a book's language (see
[Settings & Preferences](./settings)), select any word in the reader and right-click it (or
long-press on touch screens) to see its definition.

## Supported formats

Maktaba can open **EPUB** and **PDF** books directly — no extra software needed.
